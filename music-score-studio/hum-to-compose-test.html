🎚 Match the score to my humming

Original hum plays your actual recording. Play detected melody follows measured pitches and timing using an instrument sound; Play written score follows rounded musical notation. Corrections are reversible with Undo.

1. Compare

Listen for notes that are too high or low, missing pauses, or timing that is too fast or slow.

2. Adjust the whole melody's pitch

If every note is too high or low, adjust the whole melody. For just one wrong note, select it on the stave.

3. Correct the speed and rhythm

The TEST app now estimates tempo automatically when you finish humming. Change BPM and rebuild only if the score rhythms are wrong. Natural-timing playback remains available for comparison. Rebuilding replaces edits; Undo can restore them.

Select a note or rest

Amber ? = uncertain transcription. Select a symbol to correct it. Pitch arrows move by one semitone. 6/8 tempo counts dotted crotchets. Your recording stays on this device.
