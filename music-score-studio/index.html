Music Score Studio · Quick guide

You do not need to understand musical notation to begin.

  1. Five lines: Each line and each space represents a different pitch. Click higher to make a higher note.
  2. Duration: In 4/4 time, whole = 4 beats, half = 2, quarter = 1, eighth = ½ and sixteenth = ¼. A dot adds half the value.
  3. Rest: Choose Rest and click a bar to insert silence. A bar cannot contain more duration than its time signature allows.
  4. Edit: Click any note/rest to select it. Then use Move ↑ / ↓, Change Value, or Delete. You can drag notes vertically.
  5. Draw: Select Draw to annotate the staff freehand; Undo removes the latest change.
  6. Listen: Choose Warm piano, Music box, Gentle flute, or Pure reference tone in Score setup, then press Play. The sounds are synthesised locally. A scanned score may sound wrong if notes, rhythm or rests were recognised incorrectly.
  7. Save: Save Project exports an editable JSON backup. PNG and SVG export images. MusicXML imports into compatible music programs. Print / PDF uses your browser printer.
  8. Keyboard: ←/→ select previous/next note; ↑/↓ change pitch; Del delete; Ctrl+Z undo; Space play/stop.

Scope: This version writes a single melody staff, not chords or multi-part arrangements. It uses C major / no key signature by default and allows individual sharps/flats/naturals.
