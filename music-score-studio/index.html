📖 Vocabulary and symbols from the reference pages

General terms describe how to read directions; they do not independently change the playback. Enter these terms in a lesson or discuss them with pupils.

General

con, col — with; senza — without; meno — less; più — more; poco — a little; molto — very; non troppo — not excessively; poco a poco — little by little; e, ed — and; ma — but.

Articulation

Staccato — detached; staccatissimo — very detached; portato — gently separated; tenuto — sustained or given slight weight; marcato — strongly emphasized; legato/slur — smooth connection; tie — one sustained pitch.

Musical signs

Repeat signs — repeat a section; fermata — hold longer; D.C. — return to the beginning; D.S. — return to the segno; Fine — end; 8va — perform an octave higher; multi-bar rest — silence for the printed number of bars; 1st/2nd endings — alternative bars on repeat.

Tempo

Speed words give a character and approximate pace, not a compulsory number of beats per minute. Written tempo markings such as ♩ = 88 are more specific.

Music Score Studio · Quick guide

You do not need to understand musical notation to begin.

  1. Five lines: Each line and each space represents a different pitch. Click higher to make a higher note.
  2. Duration: In 4/4 time, semibreve = 4 crotchet beats, minim = 2, crotchet = 1, quaver = ½ and semiquaver = ¼. A dot adds half the value.
  3. Rest: Choose Add Rests and click a bar to insert silence. A bar cannot contain more duration than its time signature allows.
  4. Edit: Click any note/rest to select it. Then use Move ↑ / ↓, Change Value, or Delete. You can drag notes vertically.
  5. Draw: Use Teacher Annotations to mark the staff; Annotation Undo removes the latest mark.
  6. Listen: Choose Warm piano, Music box, Gentle flute, or Pure reference tone in Score setup, then press Play. The sounds are synthesised locally. A scanned score may sound wrong if notes, rhythm or rests were recognised incorrectly.
  7. Save: Save Project exports an editable JSON backup. PNG and SVG export images. MusicXML imports into compatible music programs. Print / PDF uses your browser printer.
  8. Keyboard: ←/→ select previous/next note; ↑/↓ change pitch; Del delete; Ctrl+Z undo; Space play/stop.

Scope: This version writes a single melody staff, not chords or multi-part arrangements. It uses C major / no key signature by default and allows individual sharps/flats/naturals.
