Compare an actual hummed recording with a teacher-checked reference score. This uses the real Spotify recogniser, not seeded notes. The reference must be accurate; the result measures your singing and the recogniser together, not AI errors alone.

First, download a verified score using Download score file in Hum to Compose. Hum that tune on your phone at approximately the score's tempo, then select the recording and score file below. Choose your recording and reference score to begin. No files have been read.
