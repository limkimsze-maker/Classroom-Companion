## Upgrades highlighted in teacher communications

1. **Chinese handwriting recognition (中文手写识别).** Handwritten Chinese characters on the digital whiteboard can be recognised, supporting character demonstrations, vocabulary and sentence construction.
2. **Chinese speech-to-text (中文语音转文字).** Spoken Mandarin can be converted to Chinese text to support discussions and language modelling.
3. **Chinese Read Aloud (中文朗读).** Text on the whiteboard and extracted from supported documents can be spoken in Mandarin, with simple start/stop control and adjustable reading speed. Voice quality/availability depends on the browser and system.
4. **English Read Aloud.** Reading aloud of supported text, with adjustable speed for shared reading and classroom demonstrations.
5. **Word, PDF and PowerPoint support.** Supported files can be displayed and annotated for lesson explanations without modifying the original files.

These are the features **described in the October 2026 WhatsApp announcement**; this document does not constitute fresh end-to-end testing or a guarantee that every file format or browser voice works on every device.
