How to interpret the result

Verification writers were reserved while the decision rules were developed. This checks those rule changes; it does not establish that the upstream neural models were trained on disjoint writers.

These expressions are assembled from separately written real pen glyphs and generated operator strokes. Glyphs recur across combinations. They are simulations, rather than naturally written full expressions or independent classroom trials.

Fractions, long words, integrals and full pages need their own evaluation. The Math formulas mode was not scored here. A general 99% claim needs a fresh, independent collection of naturally written classroom expressions and an agreed definition of a correct reading.

The improvements combine independent model evidence for digits and letters, preserve complete compound units, keep uncertain symbols available for review and reuse loaded models. No benchmark glyphs were added to the trained examples or personal correction memory.
